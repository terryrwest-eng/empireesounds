What we're known for

Somebody said it won't fit

Online fitment guides give up early. A speaker a quarter inch too deep, a screen the dash wasn't cut for, a box that has to clear a spare tire well — those are the jobs we like.

We build adapter rings, modify mounting locations, and fabricate enclosures to the space you actually have. If it genuinely can't be done, you'll hear that too, before you spend anything.

  • Custom adapter rings and speaker baffles
  • Fiberglass and MDF enclosures built to the vehicle
  • Dash and panel modification for oversize screens
  • Factory harness, chime, and steering wheel control retention
