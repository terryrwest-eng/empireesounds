Notes

  • Everything runs on the device. No file leaves the phone and nothing is uploaded.
  • Stationing is measured along the centreline as drawn. Curves and spirals from LandXML and DXF are rebuilt to a 1 mm chord tolerance.
  • Offsets are signed the way plans call them: + right, − left, looking up-station.
  • Phone GPS is a few metres at best. Good enough to know the station you are standing at, not a substitute for a rover.
