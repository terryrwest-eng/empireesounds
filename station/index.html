What works where

  • Android + Chrome — USB (OTG cable) and BLE both work. USB is the steadier link.
  • Desktop Chrome / Edge — USB, BLE and a plain serial port.
  • iPhone — no browser on iOS can reach a USB or Bluetooth receiver. Phone GPS only, until this is wrapped as a native app.
  • Classic Bluetooth (SPP) — the XBee-socket Bluetooth module pairs at the operating system level and no browser can see it. Use the USB cable, a BLE module, or the WiFi NTRIP Master.
  • If your receiver already gets its own corrections — an ESP32 WiFi NTRIP Master, or a base radio — leave the NTRIP section empty and just connect. The fix quality below will show RTK all the same.

Notes

  • Everything runs on the device. No file leaves the phone and nothing is uploaded.
  • Stationing is measured along the centreline as drawn. Curves and spirals from LandXML and DXF are rebuilt to a 1 mm chord tolerance.
  • Offsets are signed the way plans call them: + right, − left, looking up-station.
  • Phone GPS is a few metres at best. Good enough to know the station you are standing at, not a substitute for a rover.
